# Avoid list

Sounds to keep out of every song, except that wildcards only follow the vocal ones.
The skill sends this list to Suno as the negative style. Edit freely, one per line.

- vocals
- singing
- rap
- spoken word
- choir
- slow ballad
- acoustic folk
- ambient
- lo-fi
- trap
- dubstep
- long intro
- long breakdown
- fade-out
